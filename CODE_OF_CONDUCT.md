# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want yottacode to be a welcoming, useful, and respectful project for
contributors and users of all backgrounds and experience levels.

As maintainers, contributors, and community members, we pledge to make
participation in the yottacode community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology choices.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections when your words or actions cause
  harm.
- Keep feedback focused on the code, docs, design, or user experience.
- Be patient with new contributors and users who are learning the project.
- Share enough context for others to reproduce bugs or understand proposals.
- Respect maintainers' time by keeping issues and pull requests focused.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Insults, personal attacks, slurs, or demeaning language.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Repeatedly pushing an argument after maintainers have made a decision.
- Trolling, baiting, or other behavior that derails technical discussion.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all yottacode project spaces, including GitHub
issues, pull requests, discussions, community chats, events, and any other space
where someone is representing the project.

It also applies when public behavior outside project spaces harms the safety or
reputation of the yottacode community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable
behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull
requests, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct.

Depending on severity and pattern, maintainers may take actions including:

- a private warning
- a public moderation note
- temporary loss of participation privileges
- permanent ban from project spaces

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and focused on
protecting the community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
maintainers at `conduct@yottadynamics.com`.

Include as much context as you can safely share: links, screenshots, timestamps,
and any prior related interactions. Maintainers will review reports as quickly
as practical and keep reporter information private except where disclosure is
required for safety or legal reasons.

For security vulnerabilities, do not use this process. Follow the private
reporting path in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
